## Limitations

- Generative answers change with the platform, model, mode, date, location, and signed-in state. Tests run signed out through a US connection with a Chicago, Illinois IP address, in a new conversation for each prompt; the sample shows what the platforms answered on the test dates and does not measure visibility, share of answers, or traffic.
- Server logs and analytics are outside the agreed scope, so actual AI crawler visits and how often answers cite the website cannot be verified.
