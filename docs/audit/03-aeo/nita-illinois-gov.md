## Limitations

- Search features (“People also ask”, AI Overviews, autocomplete) were captured through a US connection with a Chicago, Illinois IP address; Google returned US results that were not localized to Chicago, and results vary by date, device, and personalization. The captures are qualitative and carry no search volume.
- Search Console and analytics are outside the agreed scope, so actual queries, featured-snippet ownership over time, and clicks cannot be measured.
