# **NITA – Website Audit & Digital Optimization Assessment**

**Client:** DHD Marketing  
**End Client:** NITA  
**Websites:** nita.illinois.gov \| mytransitbenefit.com

## 1\. Project Overview

Following the recent updates to NITA’s digital platforms, DHD Marketing is looking for an independent assessment of both websites to identify opportunities for improvement, optimize the user experience, and ensure alignment with current digital standards and best practices.  
Zebra Studio proposes a comprehensive website audit covering five key areas: **Digital Accessibility & ADA Compliance, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and User Experience (UX).**  
The objective is to provide DHD and NITA with an independent, actionable assessment of their current digital presence, highlighting areas that require attention and identifying opportunities for future improvements.  
The audit will focus on the existing websites, combining automated analysis with expert manual reviews of representative pages, templates, and user journeys.

## 2\. Scope of Work

The audit will cover both websites and include the following areas.

### **2.1. ADA Compliance & Digital Accessibility**

An accessibility assessment to identify potential barriers and evaluate alignment with applicable accessibility standards.  
The review will include:

* Evaluation against WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility guidelines.  
* Review of navigation, keyboard accessibility, and screen reader compatibility.  
* Analysis of color contrast, typography, and visual accessibility.  
* Review of images, alternative text, links, forms, and interactive elements.  
* Evaluation of semantic HTML structure and accessibility attributes.  
* Identification of accessibility issues across desktop and mobile experiences.  
* Assessment of potential compliance risks related to ADA Title II requirements.

**Special attention will be given to the upcoming ADA Title II compliance requirements**, with the goal of identifying critical issues that may require immediate remediation.  
Findings will be classified according to their severity and potential impact, helping DHD and NITA prioritize corrective actions.

### **2.2. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**

A technical and structural SEO assessment to identify issues affecting search engine visibility and organic performance.  
The review will include:

* Technical SEO analysis and website crawlability.  
* Indexability and search engine accessibility.  
* Page titles, meta descriptions, and heading structure.  
* URL structure and internal linking.  
* Sitemap and robots.txt configuration.  
* Structured data and schema markup.  
* Page performance and Core Web Vitals.  
* Mobile SEO considerations.  
* Content organization and keyword optimization opportunities.  
* Identification of technical issues that may affect organic visibility.

The objective is to provide actionable recommendations to improve search engine discoverability and overall SEO performance.

### **2.3. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)**

An assessment of how effectively the websites’ content is structured to appear in direct-answer search experiences.  
The review will include:

* Content structure and information hierarchy.  
* Identification of opportunities for featured snippets and direct answers.  
* Evaluation of question-based content and FAQ opportunities.  
* Semantic content organization.  
* Structured data opportunities.  
* Content clarity and relevance for answer-oriented search queries.

Recommendations will focus on improving the ability of search engines and answer platforms to identify and present relevant information from the websites.

### **2.4. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**

An evaluation of how effectively AI-powered search and generative platforms can discover, interpret, and reference website content.  
The assessment will consider platforms such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google Gemini.  
The review will include:

* Content accessibility for AI-powered search systems.  
* Semantic structure and content organization.  
* Structured data and machine-readable information.  
* Content clarity, authority, and contextual relevance.  
* Opportunities to improve content visibility in AI-generated answers.  
* Recommendations to improve the likelihood of content being accurately retrieved, summarized, and referenced by generative search platforms.

The objective is to identify practical improvements that support visibility in emerging AI-driven search experiences.

### **2.5. Design & User Experience (UX)**

An independent evaluation of the current website experience, focusing on usability, navigation, and overall design effectiveness.  
The review will include:

* Navigation structure and information architecture.  
* Visual hierarchy and content presentation.  
* User journeys and clarity of key actions.  
* Mobile responsiveness and usability.  
* Consistency across pages and interface elements.  
* Calls to action and user engagement opportunities.  
* Content readability and accessibility.  
* Identification of friction points and usability improvements.

The audit will focus on practical recommendations that can enhance the existing experience without necessarily requiring a complete redesign.

## 3\. Audit Methodology

The assessment will combine automated analysis tools with manual reviews performed by our team.  
Rather than conducting an exhaustive page-by-page evaluation, we will focus on representative pages, templates, and key user journeys to identify recurring issues, structural weaknesses, and meaningful improvement opportunities.  
The process will be organized into three stages.

### **Phase 1 – Initial Assessment**

* Review of both websites and their overall structure.  
* Identification of key pages, templates, and user journeys.  
* Initial automated scans for accessibility, SEO, and performance.  
* Selection of representative pages and components for detailed analysis.  
* Identification of potential critical issues.

### **Phase 2 – Detailed Audit**

* Manual and automated accessibility evaluations.  
* Technical SEO and performance assessments.  
* AEO and GEO content evaluations.  
* UX and design reviews.  
* Documentation of findings and supporting evidence.  
* Classification of identified issues according to severity, impact, and recommended priority.

### **Phase 3 – Reporting & Recommendations**

* Consolidation of findings across all audit categories.  
* Preparation of a comprehensive audit report.  
* Prioritization of recommendations.  
* Identification of quick wins and more substantial improvements.  
* Development of a recommended action plan.  
* Presentation of findings to the DHD team.  
* Discussion of potential next steps and remediation priorities.

## 4\. Deliverables

At the conclusion of the engagement, Zebra Studio will provide a comprehensive audit report covering both websites.  
The report will include:  
**Executive Summary**  
A high-level overview of the most significant findings, risks, and opportunities identified during the assessment.  
**Detailed Audit Findings**  
A structured review of each website across the five evaluation areas, including identified issues, observations, and recommendations.  
**Accessibility & Compliance Assessment**  
A dedicated section highlighting accessibility issues, their severity, and their relevance to upcoming ADA Title II requirements.  
**Prioritized Recommendations**  
A consolidated list of recommended improvements, categorized by priority:

* **Critical:** Issues requiring immediate attention, particularly accessibility and compliance risks.  
* **High:** Significant issues affecting usability, performance, or discoverability.  
* **Medium:** Improvements that can meaningfully enhance the digital experience.  
* **Low:** Minor enhancements and longer-term optimization opportunities.

**Recommended Action Plan**  
A suggested roadmap organizing improvements into immediate, short-term, and longer-term actions.  
Recommendations will include an estimated implementation complexity (Low, Medium, or High) to help DHD and NITA evaluate priorities and plan future work.

**Findings Presentation**  
One remote meeting with the DHD team to review the audit results, discuss recommendations, and address questions.  
The deliverable will be provided as a structured PDF report, supported by screenshots, examples, and technical references where appropriate.

## 5\. Technical Considerations

Both websites currently operate within an environment that includes:

* Craft CMS  
* PHP / MySQL  
* Twig templates  
* Tailwind CSS  
* Google Tag Manager

The initial audit will be conducted externally, without requiring administrative access to the CMS, hosting infrastructure, or application source code.  
Where applicable, recommendations will consider the existing technology stack to ensure they are practical and compatible with the current implementation.  
Access to analytics data may be requested if additional information is needed to support specific findings.

## 8\. Assumptions & Exclusions

The proposal is based on the following considerations:

* The audit covers the two websites identified in this proposal.  
* The assessment will be performed using publicly accessible pages and resources.  
* Backend access, source code, and server-level configurations are not included in the initial evaluation.  
* Accessibility testing will combine automated tools and manual reviews of representative pages, templates, and interactions.  
* The assessment is intended to identify accessibility issues and support remediation planning; it does not constitute a legal certification or guarantee of ADA compliance.  
* SEO, AEO, and GEO recommendations will be based on observable technical and content characteristics. Search rankings, AI citations, and traffic improvements cannot be guaranteed.  
* Implementation of recommended improvements is not included.  
* Website redesign, development, content creation, and ongoing optimization are excluded.  
* Third-party tools or services requiring paid subscriptions are not included unless explicitly agreed upon.  
* A complete page-by-page accessibility certification or exhaustive testing of every possible user interaction is outside the proposed scope.

Any implementation or remediation work resulting from the audit may be addressed through a separate proposal.

## 9\. Potential Next Steps

The audit is intended to serve as a foundation for future improvements across NITA’s digital platforms.  
Based on the findings, DHD and NITA will be able to evaluate potential initiatives such as:

* Accessibility remediation and compliance improvements.  
* Technical SEO and performance optimization.  
* Content restructuring for AEO and GEO.  
* UX and navigation enhancements.  
* Additional digital functionality and interactive experiences.

These initiatives are not part of the current scope and will be considered independently following completion of the audit.
