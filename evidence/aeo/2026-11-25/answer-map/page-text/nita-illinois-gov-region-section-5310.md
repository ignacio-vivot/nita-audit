# Section 5310 {w=0}

The Section 5310 Enhanced Mobility of Seniors and Individuals with Disabilities Program is a federal program that aims to improve mobility for seniors and individuals with disabilities by removing barriers to transportation service and expanding transportation mobility options.

## Human Services Transportation Plan {w=47}

The 2026 Human Services Transportation Plan (HSTP), released by NITA's predecessor agency the RTA, establishes a coordinated regional strategy to improve mobility for older adults, individuals with disabilities, and people with low incomes across northeastern Illinois. The HSTP identifies transportation needs, outlines priorities and strategies, and helps guide future investments in human services transportation throughout the region.

The 2026 HSTP was developed through extensive stakeholder and public engagement, including coordination with transportation providers, human service agencies, local governments, and community members. The plan evaluates existing transportation conditions, identifies unmet mobility needs, and advances recommendations focused on improving coordination, expanding access, modernizing technology, and enhancing regional connectivity. Key goals in the plan include:
- Expanding countywide and regional coordination of transportation services
- Conducting a regional Dial-A-Ride coordination study
- Improving paratransit subsidy programs
- Modernizing trip scheduling and real-time customer information systems
- Expanding access to jobs, healthcare, and wellness services
- Enhancing travel training and rider education programs

The HSTP also supports implementation of federal transportation programs, including Section 5310 funding, and aligns with broader regional coordination efforts under the NITA framework. The full plan is available below.

Federal/Local Match Process

Applicants are required to provide a reasonable cost estimate for the project and demonstrate the ability to fund the project at the time of applying. An operating project, whether classified as a traditional Section 5310 project or not, must provide a 50% local funding match in accordance with the HSTP. Capital and mobility management projects require a 20% local match. All of the local match must be provided from sources other than Federal DOT funds. For examples of other potential federal sources of local match, please view the [link:https://www.transit.dot.gov/regulations-and-programs/ccam/about/coordinating-council-access-and-mobility-ccam-federal-fu] CCAM Federal Fund Braiding Guide. Examples of sources of local match that may be used include the following:

- State or local appropriations
- Other non-DOT Federal funds
- Dedicated tax revenues
- Private donations
- Revenue from human service contracts
- Net income generated from advertising and concessions

Farebox revenue may not be used as local match. Farebox revenue is considered income and is deducted from total operating cost to determine the net cost of the activity.
