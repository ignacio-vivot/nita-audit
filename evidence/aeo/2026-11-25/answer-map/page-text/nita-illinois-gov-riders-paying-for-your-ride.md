# Paying for your ride {w=0}

Paying for transit should be affordable and seamless. NITA, CTA, Metra, and Pace are working to make it easier and cheaper for riders to take advantage of the region’s transit system.
The [link:https://metra.com/rdp] Regional Day Pass (RDP) can be purchased only in the Ventra app. Riders can purchase a Metra Day Pass with their zone of choice and add on a CTA/Pace Day Pass.

## How to pay to ride transit {w=65}

You can pay for transit with a credit card, debit card, or your smart phone. Ventra is the region’s fare payment system, which makes it faster and easier to board and pay. Ventra Cards and passes can be added to an iPhone, Apple Watch, or to an Android device with Google Wallet. New Ventra Cards and disposable Single-Ride or 1-Day Tickets are available at every Ventra vending machine and retailers around the region.

Metra calculates fares based on distance, using a zone system. Check your zone on [link:https://metra.com/fares#Fare_Calculator] Metra's website to calculate the cost of your trip. You can buy Metra tickets and passes at Metra vending machines, in the Ventra app or pay conductors on board the train at outlying stations.

## How much will my ride cost? {w=190}

The cost of your ride will depend on day, distance, type of transit, and whether or not you qualify for reduced cost transit. Explore the information below to find out how much your ride will cost.

## Metra Passes {w=752}

Metra offers Day Passes and Monthly Unlimited Ride Passes for unlimited rides in a calendar month.

### Day Passes {w=768}

Day Passes are good for unlimited rides all day between selected zones. Passes expire at 3 a.m. the next day. Five day passes that can be used on any five days within a 90-day period and are available only in the Ventra app. 
Day Pass holders can also purchase the Regional Day Pass, offering unlimited rides on CTA and Pace. The Regional Day Pass can be purchased only in the Ventra app.
Effective February 1, 2024.

### Weekend Pass {w=967}

The [link:https://metra.com/fares#Weekend_Passes] $10 Weekend Pass is good for unlimited rides on all Metra lines all day until 3 a.m. the next day following the weekend. Passes can be purchased only in the Ventra app.

### Monthly Passes {w=1001}

Monthly passes are valid for unlimited rides in a calendar month. Passes are available for purchase from the 20th to the 12th of each month. They expire at noon the first business day of the next month.
Monthly pass holders can also purchase the [link:https://metra.com/regional-connect-pass] Regional Connect Pass for $30, offering unlimited rides on CTA and Pace, with no time or day restrictions, for an entire month. The Regional Connect pass can be purchased only in the [link:https://www.ventrachicago.com/how-to/app-metra-tickets/] Ventra app. See [link:https://www.transitchicago.com/fares] transitchicago.com and [link:https://metra.com/rdp] metra.com for further details.
Effective February 1, 2024.

### Regional Connect Pass {w=1315}

Metra monthly pass holders can also purchase the Regional Connect Pass for $30. This offers unlimited rides on CTA and Pace, with no time or day restrictions, for an entire month. [ACCORDION
 : Children]

Children pay reduced fares on CTA, Metra and Pace based on their age. On CTA, children under 7 ride free with a fare-paying customer. On Metra and Pace, children under the age of 7 are not permitted to travel without a fare paying customer.

[TABLE]

 | | 
Ages 7-11 | 
Under 7

 | CTA | Reduced Fare | Free with fare-paying customer

 | Pace | Reduced Fare | Free with fare-paying adult*

 | Metra | Half Fare | Free with fare-paying adult**

[/TABLE]

* Up to two children free per adult.
** Up to three children free per adult; on weekends children age 7-11 are free

Paying for Children with Ventra

CTA and Pace Bus: Tell the bus driver you are paying for a child before you pay. Bus drivers will press a special button to put the reader to put it in a special mode where the next fare deducted is a reduced fare.

CTA Rail: Ask the ‘L’ station attendant for assistance. The ‘L’ station attendant will touch a special card to the reader in a special mode where the next fare deducted is a reduced fare. A Ventra Card can be used to pay for any combination of up to 7 riders, regardless of whether they're Full Fare or qualifying Reduced Fare riders.

Visit [link:http://transitchicago.com] transitchicago.com for more information. 

Metra Family Fares

The Metra Family Fares program allows up to three children, 11 years old and under, to ride for free with one fare-paying adult. This program is valid through Labor Day. See [link:https://metra.com/fares#Reduced_Fares_for_Seniors__Students__Military__etc_] Metra.com for further details.

[/ACCORDION]

[ACCORDION
 : Students]

CTA operates the Student Reduced Fare programs for children ages 7-20. Students obtain permits through their schools or on the [link:http://www.transitchicago.com/travel_information/fares/reduced.aspx#students] CTA website. Many college and universities participate in the [link:https://www.transitchicago.com/upass/] U-Pass program which provides unlimited rides to full-time students. Find out if your school participates [link:http://www.transitchicago.com/upass] here.

 [link:https://www.pacebus.com/student-fare-information] Pace student reduced fares  are valid weekdays for student travel to and from school activities. Pace provides the [link:https://www.pacebus.com/campus-connection] Campus Connection Pass for college students. This pass is valid for unlimited rides on Pace regular fixed route buses and On Demand every day of the week at any time of the day. [link:https://www.pacebus.com/student-fare-information] Additional Pace student fare information is available on the Pace website.

Metra allows full-time students enrolled in an accredited grade school or high school to purchase a reduced one-way, 10-ride or monthly pass. Information on purchasing student tickets is available on the [link:https://metra.com/fares#Reduced_Fares_for_Seniors__Students__Military__etc_] Metra website.

[ACCORDION
 : NICTD (South Shore Line)]

South Shore Line fares are based on distance traveled. There are four basic ticket types: One-Way, 10-Ride, 25-Ride, and Monthly tickets. Reduced Fares are available for Senior Citizens and Customers with Disabilities. Please note that Metra Tickets and Passes are not valid on [link:http://mysouthshoreline.com] South Shore Line trains. Visit [link:https://www.mysouthshoreline.com/] mysouthshoreline.com for NICTD fare options and details.
