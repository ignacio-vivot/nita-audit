One free app and one text number for CTA, Metra, and Pace.

## What is NITA Ride Safe? {w=16}

NITA Ride Safe lets you report safety concerns on any train, bus, or platform in the region, without making a phone call. Whether you ride CTA, Metra, or Pace, it works the same way. Each report goes to the right responders, so you can speak up quickly and quietly, wherever you ride. NITA Ride Safe is designed for time-sensitive safety concerns that need a prompt response. For routine customer service questions, including delays, lost items and fares, riders should continue using CTA, Metra and Pace's existing customer service channels.
No call needed
Report by app or text without drawing attention.
Flash turns off automatically
When you take a photo in the app, your camera flash stays off.
Report anonymously
You can choose not to share your name.
Works without a signal
If you’re in a tunnel or underground station, your report is saved and sent as soon as you’re back online.

### 911 {w=171}

- Someone is hurt or in immediate danger
- A weapon is being used
- A fire is spreading
- A crime is in progress and you can call safely

### NITA Ride Safe {w=197}

When you can’t safely or discreetly call, or when a concern is ongoing but not life-threatening, such as:
[TABLE]

- Harassment 
- Threatening or disruptive behavior 
- A suspicious or unattended item | 
- Drug use

- A person in crisis

[/TABLE]

For delays, lost items, and fare questions. Contact customer service for your service– [link:https://www.transitchicago.com/] CTA, [link:https://www.metra.com/] Metra, [link:https://www.pacebus.com/] Pace

## How it works {w=250}

 1

Open the Ride Safe app and tap Report an Issue, or text 312.313.9015.

 2

On the app, choose what you’re seeing from a short list. You don’t need to know how serious it is. Just tell us what you see.

 3

Add your location, a short description, or a photo or video if you can.

 4

Tap send. Your report goes to the right team, whether that’s police, fire, EMS, human services, or customer service.

## Frequently Asked Questions {w=449}

Is NITA Ride Safe free?
Yes. The app is free to download and use. Standard message and data rates may apply for texting.
Will people around me know I’m reporting?
No. You can report by app or text without making a call, and your camera flash turns off automatically.
Can I report anonymously?
Yes. You can choose to send your report without sharing your name.
Who reads my message and responds? 
Metra’s Multimedia Analytic Center (MAC) has trained staff to review reports, follow up with riders if they need more details, and send it to the right emergency or non-emergency responders
What if I don’t have a signal?
Your report will be saved and sent automatically as soon as you’re connected again.
Does NITA Ride Safe replace 911?
No. Always call 911 for life-threatening emergencies happening now.
Already use Metra COPS?
Metra COPS is now NITA Ride Safe. Your app will update on its own, and no action is needed. You’ll have the same easy reporting, now on CTA and Pace, too.
Do I need to share my location?
Your location is shared if location services are on, and you can also select your location in the app. This helps responders find you.
Not sure which option to choose?
Pick the one that’s closest to what you see. Responders review every report and take it from there.
