# People Over Parking Act {w=0}

An element of the Northern Illinois Transit Authority (NITA) Act is the creation of the People Over Parking Act (POPA), a new Illinois law effective June 1, 2026. The new law no longer allows municipalities to impose minimum parking requirements for new developments located near transit stations and high-frequency bus routes.
Learn more and [link:https://rtagis.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/lookup/index.html?appid=ba44a9b4220f43aeb2e42b8f63f7f5e3] view a map on this page or at [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/popamap] https://nita.illinois.gov/popamap.

## Explore POPA in Your Community {w=73}

To support implementation of POPA, NITA staff, in close coordination with Metra, CTA, Pace and Chicago Metropolitan Agency for Planning (CMAP) staff, created a mapping tool depicting all locations in the region to which the Act applies. Explore the interactive map and read more in the [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/uploads/files/general/About-RTA/PeopleOverParkingAct_PolicyApproach_WithAppendices_10.14.2026-] methodology document. 
These resources are intended to assist municipalities to align their parking regulations with POPA; the map is an interactive tool that municipalities can use to understand where new developments would be subject to POPA and subsequently revise parking regulations in those locations.

## Apply for NITA Technical Assistance {w=167}

NITA is offering technical assistance for municipalities to amend parking regulations in transit served areas as outlined in the People Over Parking Act (POPA). POPA no longer allows municipalities to impose minimum parking requirements for new developments located near transit stations and high-frequency bus routes, specifically to new development projects located one-half mile from a Public Transportation Hub or one-eighth of a mile from a Public Transportation Corridor. Excessive off-street parking requirements often lead to higher development costs near transit, under-utilized land of high value near transit, and a decrease in walkability.

## How to obtain assistance {w=264}

Complete the [link:https://nita.formstack.com/forms/parking_regulation_application] interest form.
While there is no due date, NITA encourages municipalities interested in this technical assistance to complete the form as soon as possible. Applicants can also save a draft of their form by clicking save and resume later.
If you would like help with filling out an interest form, please email [link:mailto:applications@nita.illinois.gov] applications@nita.illinois.gov.

### Type of assistance being offered {w=322}

NITA will assist municipalities in rewriting existing off-street parking requirements to comply with POPA. The process will include reviewing existing regulations, reviewing areas within the municipality impacted by POPA, creating a strategy for POPA compliance, and drafting revisions to parking regulations accordingly. Assistance will include support needed to adopt the revised parking regulations.
In addition to the assistance provided to municipalities to amend parking regulations to adhere to POPA, NITA is offering municipalities assistance to amend parking regulations that support transit-supportive development, such as establishing parking maximums, creating bicycle parking requirements, or to develop a parking management policy. 
If requested, NITA will also consider providing additional transit-area zoning support to local governments that are interested in:
- Updating permitted / allowable use lists
- Reducing allowed automotive-oriented land uses in zones with high-frequency transit service
- Creating a new district (e.g., overlay or mixed-use) that overlaps with areas served by high-frequency transit
- Increasing allowable density in transit-served areas
- Building requirements (e.g. set-backs, bulk, etc.) that advance walkability
- New or improved design guidelines
- Streamline the permit approval / development review processes
Final deliverables for assistance offered may include a revised zoning ordinance, subdivision ordinance, unified development ordinance, overlay district, or other regulations if the municipality is requesting transit-supportive zoning assistance in addition to assistance with parking regulations.

### Project Selection Process {w=545}

NITA staff will assess inquiries for technical assistance based on the following criteria: the municipality’s level of transit services, the geographic scope of areas impacted by POPA, the municipality’s intent to amend existing parking regulations, and the level of support among elected officials, appointed officials, and members of the public, (expressed during public hearings, committee meetings, and meetings of elected officials). 

### Frequently Asked Questions {w=608}

Can I preview the questions before submitting an interest form online?
Yes. Applicants can save a draft of their form by clicking save and resume later.
Who is eligible to receive assistance?
Municipalities throughout northeastern Illinois in the counties of Cook, DuPage, Kane, Lake, McHenry and Will.
Is there a due date to submit the interest form?
This technical assistance is being offered until further notice, with inquiries evaluated on a rolling basis. While there is no due date, NITA encourages municipalities interested in this technical assistance to complete the form as soon as possible.
When will interested municipalities be notified?
NITA will review interest forms on a rolling basis as they are submitted. Applicants will be contacted via email to provide further information if needed and will be notified about the request status within 30 days of submission.
How will projects be evaluated?
Proposed projects are reviewed by NITA staff members according to the following criteria: the municipality’s level of transit services, the geographic scope of areas impacted by POPA, the municipality’s intent to amend existing parking regulations, and the level of support among elected officials, appointed officials, and members of the public, (expressed during public hearings, committee meetings, and meetings of elected officials).
Is there a local match or a required program contribution?
No, NITA will not require a local match and will fund the costs of these projects in their entirety.
Is NITA offering municipalities staff support or consultant assistance?
NITA anticipates utilizing consultant assistance to support these projects
What is the anticipated timeframe for projects to begin?
NITA anticipates scope development and consultant procurement to begin within 90 days of requests being submitted.
Who will be responsible for managing the project?
A NITA staff member or representative will be assigned to each project to assist with project development and management. The local partner will be responsible for project management that meets internal municipal needs and processes. NITA will be responsible for consultant contract administration and invoicing.
What type of agreement needs to be signed before a project begins?
NITA requires a Memorandum of Understanding to be fully executed before starting a project.
Who do I contact if I still have questions?
Please contact [link:mailto:applications@nita.illinois.gov] applications@nita.illinois.gov.
