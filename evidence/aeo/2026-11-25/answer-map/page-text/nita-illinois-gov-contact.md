## Individuals Needing Reasonable Accommodation {w=116}

NITA will provide reasonable auxiliary aids or services necessary to afford an individual with a disability an equal opportunity to use the services of the agency. Persons requiring assistance are requested to notify NITA by completing the form to the right with details about the necessary accommodations.

## Office of Executive Inspector General {w=180}

Reports of fraud, waste and abuse within NITA, CTA, Metra and Pace, or any of their vendors, may be made directly with the Office of Executive Inspector General via the toll-free Hotline ( [link:tel:8668141113] 866-814-1113) or TDD ( [link:tel:8882612734] 888-261-2734), online at [link:https://oeig.illinois.gov/] https://oeig.illinois.gov/, by mail or in-person at either of the OEIG's two offices (69 W. Washington, Suite 3400, Chicago, IL 60602 or 607 E. Adams, 14th floor, Springfield, IL 62701). All complaints are confidential and you may remain anonymous if you wish.

[/HIDDEN-PANEL]

## Title VI Program and Complaint Form {w=269}

Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 provides that no person in the United States shall, on the ground of race, color, or national origin, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be subjected to discrimination under, any program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance. Read more about the [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/sites/default/files/documents/aboutus/Title%20VI%20Program%202020%20FINAL.pdf] NITA Title VI Program (Notice available in Spanish - [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/sites/default/files/documents/aboutus/Spanish-Title-VI-Notice-to-Public.pdf] "Aviso de derechos del Titulo VI al publico.")
File a complaint using the  [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/sites/default/files/documents/aboutus/Title%20VI%20Complaint%20Form.pdf] NITA Title VI Complaint Form (Available in Spanish - [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/sites/default/files/documents/aboutus/Spanish-Title-VI-Complaint-Form.pdf] "Formulario de queja del Titulo VI.")
