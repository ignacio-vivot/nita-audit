# About NITA {w=0}

The Northern Illinois Transit Authority (NITA), formerly the Regional Transportation Authority, was created by the Illinois General Assembly and Governor JB Pritzker to make transit across northeastern Illinois work for riders as one regional transit system. 

NITA sets regional fares, service standards, and schedules across the [link:http://www.transitchicago.com] Chicago Transit Authority (CTA), [link:https://metra.com/] Metra, and  [link:https://www.pacebus.com/] Pace; plans and funds transit investment; and oversees system performance and finances. Across the six-county region, the transit system provides more than 1.3 million rides a day.

## Leadership {w=82}

The Northern Illinois Transit Authority is governed by the [link:https://ilga.gov/Documents/Legislation/PublicActs/104/PDF/104-0457.pdf] NITA Act and [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/sites/default/files/documents/aboutus/16-RTA%20Board%20By-Laws.pdf] by-laws the agency is overseen by a 20-member [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/about-nita/boards-and-committees/board-of-directors] Board of Directors. Visit the [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/about-nita/boards-and-committees/board-of-directors] Board and Committees page and [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/blog/2026/09/18/ad-hoc-committee-on-the-transition-to-nita-issues-final-report] read a blog and report summarizing recommendations from the Ad Hoc Committee on Transition to NITA to learn more.

## Doing business with NITA {w=133}

NITA's Procurement and Contracting department is responsible for procuring goods, services and commodities on behalf of NITA. NITA seeks to do business with experienced and responsible firms utilizing an open, transparent, and competitive process to procure products and services.
NITA standards incorporate best industry practices that promote accountability and efficiency in our procurement processes
