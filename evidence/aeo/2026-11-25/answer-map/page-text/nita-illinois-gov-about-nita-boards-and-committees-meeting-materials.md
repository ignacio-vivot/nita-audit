# Meeting Materials {w=0}

Find materials for the NITA Board of Directors and committees past and present on this page. 
All NITA Board meetings take place at 9:00 a.m. in the Board Room, 16th Floor, 175 W. Jackson Blvd., Chicago, IL 60604
Email  [link:mailto:communications@rtachicago.org] communications@nita.illinois.gov by noon the day prior to comment at a meeting. Call NITA  [link:tel:3129133219] (312) 913-3219 to request accommodations for individuals with disabilities.
