# Board of Directors {w=0}

NITA is governed by a 20-member Board of Directors that meets on the third Thursday of each month. Board member bios can be found below. Visit the [link:https://nita.illinois.gov/about-nita/boards-and-committees/meeting-materials] Meeting Materials page to view meeting livestreams, agendas, archived materials, and more.

## Members {w=40}

To assure regional representation, 20 directors are appointed from within the six-county region: five by the Mayor of the City of Chicago; five by the Governor of Illinois, five by the Cook County Board; and five by the Chairman of each collar county, with one representative each from DuPage, Kane, Lake, McHenry, and Will.

NITA Board Chair Tom Kotarac is Senior Vice President for Transportation and Infrastructure at the Civic Committee of the Commercial Club of Chicago, where he leads the organization’s work to strengthen the Chicago region’s transportation system, infrastructure, and economic competitiveness.
Previously, Kotarac served as Deputy Executive Director of the Chicago Metropolitan Agency for Planning (CMAP), helping lead the region’s comprehensive planning agency and its work on transportation, land use, and economic development. Earlier in his career, he spent more than 12 years on Capitol Hill serving the Illinois congressional delegation, including as senior policy advisor and projects director for U.S. Senator Richard Durbin.
Kotarac served on the Regional Transportation Authority Board and Chaired the RTA Subcommittee focused on the transition to the Northern Illinois Transit Authority.

A Chicago native and a fluent Spanish speaker, he earned his Master's in Social Policy from the University of Chicago and a Bachelor in Economics and Political Science from the University of Wisconsin-Madison.

With over 30 years of leadership in public transportation at the Chicago Transit Authority (CTA), Romayne C. Brown has built a distinguished career dedicated to advancing safe, reliable, and efficient transit systems. During a three-decade tenure with CTA, Romayne served in a variety of executive leadership roles, culminating as Vice President of Rail Operations, where she oversaw one of the nation's largest urban rail networks and led initiatives to improve operational performance, safety, and customer service.
In addition to her CTA leadership, Romayne has served for 13 years on the Metra Board of Directors, including servicing 4-years as Chair, where she continues to provide strategic governance and oversight for one of the country's largest commuter rail systems. Throughout this role, Romayne has helped guide policy, capital investment, financial stewardship, and long-term planning to strengthen regional mobility.
Leveraging more than three decades of experience in transportation and public administration, Romayne is recognized for collaborative leadership, operational excellence, and a deep commitment to serving the public. Romayne’s career reflects a proven ability to lead complex organizations, foster strong partnerships, and deliver transportation solutions that support economic growth and improve quality of life for all the communities they serve.

Brian Sager was appointed to represent McHenry County on the RTA Board of Directors in June 2018 and appointed the county’s representative to the NITA and Metra Boards in July 2026. Dr. Sager worked for the University of Illinois Cooperative Extension Service as Agricultural Adviser in Vermilion and McHenry Counties for a total of 12 years. Between those posts he was appointed Far East Trade Representative in Hong Kong for the Illinois Department of Agriculture. He taught as a Professor of Animal and Plant Sciences and Economics at McHenry County College for 18 years and subsequently served as Vice President of Academic and Student Affairs and Acting President of the College. Upon retiring from MCC, he served as Dean of Social Sciences and Humanities, Vice President of Academic Affairs and later Provost of Rock Valley College in Rockford, Illinois.

Dr. Sager entered local politics when first elected as a Woodstock City Councilman in 1989 and was elected Mayor of Woodstock in 2005, serving four terms and retiring as the longest-serving mayor in the city’s history. He continues his work with the city as Coordinator of Sister City Programs with the State of Zacatecas, Mexico. Following municipal service, he was elected to a four-year term on the McHenry County Board.

Brian received his B.S. degree in animal and plant sciences and M.S. degree in agricultural economic development from the University of Illinois. He earned his Ph.D. in international development from Louisiana State University and an M.A. in instructional strategies from Rockford University.  Having started his career in agriculture, Dr. Sager joined the Livestock Leadership Team of the Illinois State Fair and retired as General Livestock Superintendent in 2025 after 50 years of service. As a certified auctioneer, he volunteers to conduct charity auctions for county and regional non-profits.
