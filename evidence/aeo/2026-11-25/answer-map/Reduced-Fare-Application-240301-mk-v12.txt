The Reduced Fare Permit allows people age 65 or over and individuals with disabilities
under the age of 65 to ride at a reduced rate on Metra, Pace, and CTA bus and
rail service.

renewal, or replacement Reduced Fare Permit
within 7-10 business days. The online portal works
