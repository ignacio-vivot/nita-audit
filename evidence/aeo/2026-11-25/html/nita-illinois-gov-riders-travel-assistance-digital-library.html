Access Program

NITA, Metra, and Cook County have partnered to extend reduced fares to Metra riders experiencing low incomes regionwide through the Access Program. Stay tuned for news as the program gets expanded Fall 2026.

All recipients of Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program, or SNAP, living in the counties of Cook, DuPage, Kane, Lake, McHenry, and Will are eligible to apply for more affordable transit at GetAccess.org.

  • Access Pilot Program Application (Spanish)
  • Access Pilot Program Brochure (English)
  • Access Pilot Program Brochure (Spanish)
 Learn more about Access

ADA Paratransit

Individuals who are interested in using ADA Paratransit service must apply and be found eligible according to ADA guidelines. NITA handles ADA Paratransit Certification for the entire Chicago region to determine an individual's functional ability to use fixed route services.

If you have more questions about ADA Paratransit eligibility after reviewing the documents below, call (312) 663-4357.

  • ADA Paratransit Brochure (English)
  • ADA Paratransit Brochure (Spanish)

 

 Learn more about Accessible Transit

Ride Free

Older adults and people with disabilities who are enrolled in the Illinois Department on Aging’s Benefit Access program are eligible to ride free on the CTA, Metra and Pace fixed-route services. Eligibility in the Illinois Department on Aging’s Benefit Access Program (BAP) is required in order to be found eligible for the NITA Ride Free program.

Apply for the NITA Ride Free program via the NITA Fare Programs Portal or learn more with the documents below.

  • Ride Free Application (English)
  • Ride Free Application (Spanish)
  • Ride Free Brochure (English)
  • Ride Free Brochure (Spanish)
 Learn more about Ride Free

Reduced Fare

NITA issues Reduced Fare Permits to allow older adults and persons with qualifying disabilities to ride at a reduced rate on CTA, Metra and Pace buses and trains.

Apply for the NITA Reduced Fare program via the NITA Fare Programs Portal or learn more with the documents below.

  • Reduced Fare Application
  • Reduced Fare Application (Spanish)
  • Reduced Fare Brochure (English)
  • Reduced Fare Brochure (Spanish)

Travel Training

The NITA Travel Training Program helps people with disabilities and older adults learn how to use Metra, Pace, and CTA buses and trains. Travel Training helps people become more independent in their travel.
