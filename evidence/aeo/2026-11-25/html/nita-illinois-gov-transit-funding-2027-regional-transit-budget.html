2027 NITA Regional Budget

In 2027, our promise to riders is to put your experience at the center of regional decisions. You should be able to count on transit that gets you where you need to go, connections that work, information you can trust, and services you can afford and use. This budget backs that commitment with investments in safer trips, more reliable service, and a more accessible system.

Take Action

As part of the NITA Act, there is one regional budget for 2027 encompassing plans for NITA, CTA, Metra, and Pace. Download the proposed budget, submit a comment, or register for the public hearing below.

Proposed 2027 Budget Highlights

The 2027 Regional Operating Budget contains $5.04 Billion in funding for NITA, CTA, Metra, and Pace alongside a Five-Year Capital Program for 2027–2031 of $9.73 Billion. Read the major projects and priorities funded in that budget below.

All while achieving $60.1 Million in Efficiencies. $13.2 million more than required by the NITA Act.

Capital Projects & Investments

Largest Investments in Five-Year Capital Program

Funded amounts show dollars programmed in the 5-year capital program, not necessarily the full project cost.

  • CTA Red Line Extension. Delivering 4 new accessible stations, a new rail yard and 5.5 miles of new track. $3.29B
  • CTA Purchase of New Rail Cars. Continued purchase of 7000 series rail cars and funding for next-gen 9000 series rail cars. $436M
  • CTA Purchase of New Buses. Includes articulated buses, electric, and hybrid buses. $404M
  • Metra Purchase of New Rail Cars and Trainsets. Includes the purchase of new bi-level cars and zero-emission trainsets and trailer cars. $483M
  • Metra Rail Car Rehabilitation. Rehab of Metra Electric and diesel-powered rail cars. $274M
  • Metra Van Buren Street Station. Full rehab and accessibility improvements to the historic downtown station. $156M
  • Pace Purchase of New Buses. Includes the purchase of electric, hybrid, and CNG powered buses. $218M
  • Pace Garage Improvements and Electrification. Construction of new garages in Wheeling and Elgin as well as upgrade to garages systemwide. $89M
  • Pace Pulse Cermak / 22nd St Line. Construction of BRT along Cermak and 22nd St in Cook and DuPage Counties. $42M

What Moves You? Public Engagement Shapes the 2027 Budget

NITA heard from thousands of residents across the region as part of its 2027 budget development process. The “What Moves You?” online survey received 4,368 responses, with participants prioritizing more frequent and reliable service, infrastructure investments, and public safety. Additionally, nearly 400 people attended NITA's October 14 open house at Union Station to share feedback and learn about plans for regional transit.

The feedback helped inform the Proposed 2027 Budget and will continue to guide future planning and investments.

Read more about what we heard and explore the full survey results.
