Employers

Want to offer your employees a benefit that encourages them to come into the office, helps the environment, and saves both you and them money? The NITA Transit Benefit Fare Program allows your employees to set aside pre-tax income, up to $340 each month, for parking and transit fares on CTA, Metra, and Pace. Employers save money on payroll taxes and employees are incentivized to use transit to get to work, saving them money and time. The NITA Transit Benefit Fare Program will help businesses large and small comply with the Transit Benefits Program Act, which went into effect on January 1, 2024. The new law requires employers with more than 50 full-time or part-time employees, located within a mile of fixed-route transit service in the six-county NITA region, to offer their employees a pre-tax benefit.

New law requires employers to offer transit benefits

Not only does the NITA Transit Benefit Fare Program help employers save money and attract talent, it also helps businesses and organizations comply with the new Transit Benefits Program Act, which goes into effect on January 1, 2024. The new Illinois law requires employers with more than 50 full-time or part-time employees, located within a mile of fixed-route transit service in the six-county NITA region, to offer their employees a pre-tax benefit.
