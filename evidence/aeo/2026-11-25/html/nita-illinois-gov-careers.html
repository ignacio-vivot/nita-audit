Any applicant considered for a position will be contacted by  NITA Human Resources staff. If you require an accommodation in the application or hiring process, please contact humanresources@nita.illinois.gov prior to the submission of your application or upon request for an interview. NITA will work with you to determine if an accommodation can be provided.

Notice: Use of Artificial Intelligence in Our Hiring Process

Artificial intelligence or automated technology may be used to assist in the initial review and screening of applications and resumes submitted for this position, including evaluating application materials against job-related criteria. Such technology is used as a tool to support the hiring process and does not replace human review or decision-making.

For questions regarding the use of artificial intelligence in the application process or to request a reasonable accommodation, please contact humanresources@nita.illinois.gov.

Use of Artificial Intelligence During the Interview Process

It is important that all interviews reflect the applicant’s own reasoning and experience. Applicants must therefore complete live interviews independently and may not use artificial intelligence tools to generate, suggest, or script responses. This includes text generators, real-time coaching tools, second-screen feeds, or hidden prompts. Unauthorized use of artificial intelligence to generate responses during an interview may result in disqualification from the hiring process. This provision does not prohibit assistive technology or a reasonable accommodation authorized by NITA.

Why work for NITA

We are committed to diversity as an Equal Opportunity/ Affirmative Action Employer. Our policy is to fill vacant positions with qualified candidates without regard to race, color, sex, religion, national origin, age, gender identity, disability, or any protected categories.

NITA honors and recognizes the unique skills, leadership, and experiences that veterans have gained through their service. NITA will recognize any military experience towards the minimum qualifications of a job if it is applicable per the experience requirement as stated in the job description.

Benefits

In addition to a hybrid work environment and flexible work schedules, NITA offers a competitive benefits package with:
 

  • Pension plan or 401(k) salary match provided by NITA at no cost to you
  • 401(k) and/or 457 plan options
  • Hybrid work environment and flexible work schedules
  • Transit benefits to support your commute
  • Twelve paid holidays and one floating holiday
  • Competitive personal leave package
  • Parental leave
  • Medical/dental/vision/life/pet insurance discount/AD&D insurance
  • Flexible Spending Account
  • Tuition reimbursement
  • On-site gym membership discount
  • Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) eligible employer
  • Employee Assistance Program

NITA Credo

As NITA staff, we pride ourselves on diversity, equal opportunity, and camaraderie. The agency is committed to hiring and retaining staff who uphold our credo:﻿

We believe in...

  • An organizational environment of Trust, Openness and Respect.
  • Teamwork as vital to accomplishing our mission.
  • Integrity on an organizational and personal level.
  • Service of the highest level to our customers both internal and external.
  • Development of work skills for employees at all levels.

We're committed to...

  • A high-caliber and diverse work force which will enable us to take pride in our results.
  • Self-expression with an openness to new ideas, willingness to listen, and honest communication with the ultimate goal of coming together in cooperation, mutual support and teamwork.
  • Individual empowerment by sharing responsibility, authority, support and resources which will enable the individual to be accountable for results.
  • Self-initiative to seek opportunities to grow and develop within NITA through greater participation, education, training and coaching.
  • Recognize personal creativity and innovation; and acknowledge it.
  • Conduct ourselves using the highest standard of ethical behavior.
