Whistleblower Policy

NITA has a strong commitment to conduct its business in a lawful and ethical manner. NITA's policy is designed to be in compliance with the Illinois Whistleblower Reward and Protection Act (740 ILCS 174), and this policy shall be interpreted subject to the standards in the Act. The Act protects every citizen, including state and local government employees, when they blow the whistle on government corruption. It is the practice of NITA not to allow retaliation for reports of wrongdoing, fraud, corruption and/or waste made in good faith and it is the duty of every NITA employee to report suspect improprieties. It is, at the same time, unacceptable to file a report knowing it is false. Any employee who violates this prohibition will be subject to discipline in accordance with NITA policy up to and including termination.
 

Learn more about the NITA's Whistleblower Policy.

To report any wrongdoing, fraud, corruption or waste within NITA, CTA, Metra or Pace, call (312) 913-3213 or report online using the form below.

To report to the Office of Executive Inspector General, call their toll-free hotline (866) 814-1113 or report online here.
