Ten Year Financial Plan

The Ten Year Financial Plan projects the operating results of the NITA system over the next decade, using specific assumptions for ridership, operating revenue, public funding, and expense growth. A summary of ten-year capital funding based on the most recent Capital Asset Condition Assessment is also included. The next Ten Year Financial Plan is being developed as part of the next Regional Transit Strategic Plan to be adopted in early 2023.
