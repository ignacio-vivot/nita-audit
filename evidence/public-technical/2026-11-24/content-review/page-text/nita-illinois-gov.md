 NITA Ride Safe App Launches

See something unsafe? Report it discreetly. One free app and one text number for CTA, Metra, and Pace. Available now on the App Store and Google Play.

 [Learn more](https://nita.illinois.gov/safety/app)

# 
 Weigh in on NITA's draft regional budget

NITA's first proposed regional budget expands service, creates jobs, and improves safety across six counties. Submit public comment by December 4 and register for a November 12 public hearing.

 [Learn more](https://nita.illinois.gov/transit-funding/2027-regional-transit-budget)

# 
 Municipalities can apply for assistance in adhering to POPA

NITA is offering technical assistance for municipalities to amend parking regulations in transit served areas as outlined in the People Over Parking Act.

 [Learn more](https://nita.illinois.gov/communities/people-over-parking-act)
