### 200+ communities served by transit 

With more than 200 communities in northeastern Illinois, NITA has many partners in its efforts to improve mobility. To support local governments, nonprofit organizations, and others in creating more transit-friendly communities, NITA provides land use planning support and funding opportunities to municipalities and transportation agencies across the region.

## Community Planning

The Community Planning program provides funding and technical assistance to applicants for planning projects and plan implementation activities that benefit the community and the regional transit system. The agency has provided funding and planning assistance through the Community Planning program to over 100 local governments since 1998 under the Regional Transportation Authority (RTA).

 [Learn more](https://nita.illinois.gov/communities/community-planning)

## Access to Transit

The Access to Transit program was launched in 2012 to support small-scale capital projects that improve pedestrians’ and bicyclists’ access to public transportation. 

 [Learn more](https://nita.illinois.gov/communities/access-to-transit-program)
