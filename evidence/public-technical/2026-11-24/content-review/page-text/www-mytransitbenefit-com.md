### Discover the Future of Commuting in Chicagoland!

## How the Program Works

Section 132(f) of federal tax law allows employees to pay for transit and parking costs using pre-tax dollars. By using pre-tax dollars, employees can save up to 40% annually on their commuting costs while also reducing your company’s payroll taxes.

 [Behind the benefits](/behind-the-benefits)

 Registration

Employers can sign up to enroll and begin offering this benefit to employees.

 Support

NITA staff can answer any questions about the process or provide support.

 Benefits

Participants in the program can take advantage of various products with their pre-tax benefit.

 Flexibility

The program allows any size company to add to the benefits package.

## I'm an Employer

Want to offer your employees a benefit that encourages them to come into the office, helps the environment, and saves both you and them money?

 Up to 7.65% in savings.

The NITA Transit Benefit Fare Program allows your employees to set aside pre-tax income, up to $340 each month.

## I'm an Employee

Participating in the NITA Transit Fare Benefit Program saves you money and time on your commute.

 Save 40% with pre-tax deductions.

Set aside pre-tax money for transit and parking costs to make your travel to work easier and less expensive.
If your employer doesn’t offer a transit benefit fare program, encourage them to enroll today.
